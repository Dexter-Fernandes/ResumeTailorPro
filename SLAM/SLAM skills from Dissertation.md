• Developed and implemented a novel real-time SLAM solution by integrating LiDAR-based mapping (RTAB-Map) with gamma-ray detection on an Unmanned Ground Vehicle (UGV).
• Conducted extensive tests to evaluate autonomous navigation and mapping accuracy using Velodyne VLP-16 LiDAR, achieving reliable performance in diverse environments, including indoor high-detail and outdoor feature-scarce areas.
• Optimised RTAB-Map parameters specifically for enhanced performance in varying terrain conditions, significantly improving the accuracy of localisation and mapping in challenging outdoor settings.
• Conducted simulations for 2D and 3D SLAM, as well as waypoint navigation of the Clearpath Husky robot in Gazebo, using ROS 2 Humble to validate and tune navigation algorithms in both indoor and outdoor scenarios
• Validated the system through rigorous real-world experimentation, including the deployment of the robot in complex industrial environments, effectively navigating obstacles and identifying radioactive sources in real-time.
